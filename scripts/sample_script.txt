The Boxer CRV — Australia's Armored Future

Australia's Defence Force operates one of the most advanced armored vehicle fleets in the Indo-Pacific region. At the heart of this fleet sits the Boxer Combat Reconnaissance Vehicle, or CRV.

The Boxer CRV was developed through a joint venture between Germany's Rheinmetall and the Netherlands. This 8x8 platform combines firepower, protection, and mobility in a single, highly modular package.

Australia selected the Boxer as part of its Land 400 program, replacing the aging ASLAV fleet. With over 200 vehicles planned, the Boxer CRV represents one of Australia's largest military vehicle acquisitions in decades.

The vehicle is armed with a 30mm autocannon and anti-tank missiles, giving it significant combat power. Its armored hull provides NATO Stanag Level 4 protection against mines and IEDs — critical for the kind of operations Australia's Army conducts.

Australia's Boxer fleet is being built at a facility in Queensland, supporting local jobs and defense industry capability. This joint Germany-Netherlands-Australia partnership ensures both technical excellence and sovereign capability.

The Boxer CRV is not just a vehicle. It's a statement of Australia's commitment to regional security and its alliance with partners like the United States, Britain, and other NATO nations.
