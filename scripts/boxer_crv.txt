The Boxer CRV is Australia's primary armored fighting vehicle, selected under the LAND 400 program to replace the aging ASLAV fleet.

Developed through a joint venture between Germany's Rheinmetall and the Netherlands, this 8x8 platform combines firepower, protection, and mobility in a single, highly modular package. Over 200 vehicles are planned for the Australian Army.

The vehicle is armed with a 30mm autocannon and can carry up to eight soldiers. Its modular design allows the same chassis to be configured for reconnaissance, command, ambulance, and other roles.

Australia's Boxer fleet is being built at a facility in Ipswich, Queensland. This supports local jobs and strengthens Australia's sovereign defense industrial base. The first vehicles entered service in 2019.

The Boxer CRV represents Australia's commitment to regional security in the Indo-Pacific. Its partnership with Germany and the Netherlands reflects the importance of international cooperation in modern defense.
