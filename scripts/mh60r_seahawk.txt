The MH-60R Seahawk is Australia's primary anti-submarine warfare helicopter. Operated by the Royal Australian Navy, it plays a critical role in protecting Australia's maritime approaches.

Built by Sikorsky, the MH-60R carries an advanced sonar system that can detect submarines at long range. Its Hellfire missiles and torpedoes give it the ability to engage threats directly. The helicopter's radar can track over 200 targets simultaneously.

Australia operates 24 MH-60R helicopters from its Hobart-class destroyers and Anzac-class frigates. The helicopters can also operate from the flagship HMAS Canberra and other amphibious vessels.

The Seahawk's endurance of over 5 hours allows it to conduct extended surveillance patrols over the vast Indian and Pacific Oceans. This endurance is critical for detecting submarine activity in Australia's northern approaches.

The MH-60R has been a game-changer for Australia's maritime defense. Combined with the P-8A Poseidon maritime patrol aircraft, it creates a layered anti-submarine warfare capability unmatched in the Indo-Pacific.
